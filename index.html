Method

Semantic memory

Links the avatar's parts and garments to their persistent Gaussian regions and appearance descriptions. The MLLM selects target categories from it, even when the query refers to them by appearance, relation, or function.

Motion memory and operators

Stores the avatar's joint trajectories. A registry of geometric operators, such as a point moving down, a limb moving up, or one part above another, gives the MLLM a vocabulary for motion.

Motion program

The MLLM composes operators into a motion program, and a deterministic executor runs it on the motion memory to find the matching intervals. The chosen categories and operator results keep each selection traceable.

Highlighted regions and intervals are rendered from the avatars' Gaussians using saved method outputs.
